    How to get the best look

    01

    Name owned pieces

    “I have dark navy chinos” tells the agent to skip bottoms and complete the outfit around them.

    02

    Set the room

    Occasion, weather, dress code, and mood sharpen retrieval and the LLM's pairing decision.

    03

    Add constraints

    Budget, colors, gender (“for my wife”), and “no black” rules are honored before the final look.
